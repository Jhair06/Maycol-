                                           LIFE STILE

Kevin Levrone, known as the "Maryland Muscle Machine," was a prominent figure in 1990s bodybuilding with a distinct approach to the sport. Unlike many bodybuilders who maintained a competitive physique year-round, Levrone was famous for his "growing into a show" strategy, drastically downsizing in the off-season. This allowed him to focus on other passions, including music (with his band Fulblown), acting, playing sports like golf and tennis, and pursuing a regular life outside of the gym. He is noted for his incredible genetics and ability to gain muscle quickly when preparing for competitions. His diet emphasized clean, unprocessed foods like fish, chicken, rice, and vegetables, consumed multiple times a day, particularly when preparing for contests. Levrone's training involved a high-volume, low-rep approach with basic exercises, though he also incorporated speed, agility, and balance training. He also prioritizes overal health, emphasizing check-ups and clean living.
